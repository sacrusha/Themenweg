The railway bridge was built in 1856, for the Rheinfallbahn from Winterthur to Schaffhausen, which opened in 1857. On stone arches it crosses the river at an angle, about ten metres above the water, shortly before the river drops.

People have only walked here since the end of the 1950s, when footpaths were added on both sides of the bridge. Since then the way has led across the river, with a view down onto the edge of the falls.

Today S-Bahn trains stop at both ends, at Neuhausen Rheinfall and at Schloss Laufen am Rheinfall. The railway that once opened up the falls to travellers has itself become part of the view: in many pictures since 1857 a train crosses the arches.
